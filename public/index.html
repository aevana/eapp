📋 Features

👥
Customer Management
Add, edit and track all your electricity customers in one place.
🧾
Bill Tracking
Create bills, record collections, track arrears and pending amounts.
📅
Month-wise Tracker
Filter bills by month and year to see who has paid and who hasn't.
📒
Balance Sheet
Full profit analysis with expected vs actual profit and board bill comparison.
⚡
Units Projection
Project electricity units from active days × quantity × 20 or 25 units/day.
💬
WhatsApp Alerts
Send bill notifications directly to customers via WhatsApp.
📥
CSV Import / Export
Backup and migrate data easily with CSV files.
🔒
100% Offline
All data stored on-device. No internet required, no servers involved.
🔒
Your Data Stays On Your Device
This app stores everything — customers, bills, charges — in your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever sent to any server. Your data is 100% private and under your control. Use the Backup tab regularly to export a copy.

💡 Tips & Shortcuts

  • Home Tap any running card to jump straight to that customer's detail page.
  • Tracker Select the 20 or 25 units/day toggle before hitting View to get accurate projected units.
  • Tracker Scroll down after loading the Balance Sheet to see the monthly charge details and donut charts.
  • Bills Use the Active / Stopped toggle to quickly filter running vs completed bills.
  • Backup Export a CSV backup before clearing your browser data or switching devices.
  • WhatsApp Ensure the customer's mobile number is saved with the country code for WhatsApp links to work.
  • CSV You can import customers, bills, and monthly charges from separate CSV files — match column headers exactly.
